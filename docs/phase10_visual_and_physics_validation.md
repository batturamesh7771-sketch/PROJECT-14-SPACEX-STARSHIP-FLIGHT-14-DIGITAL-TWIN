## 1. Physical and Numerical Grounding

Every visual element and telemetry readout rendered in the dashboard is directly bound to verified outputs generated by the project's numerical physics engines:

1. **Ascent & Powered Flight (Phase 3 Solver):**
   - Integrator: 3-DOF Runge-Kutta 4th order (RK4) with variable pitch profile $\theta(t)$.
   - Vehicle mass depletion: $m(t) = m_0 - \int \dot{m}_{prop} dt$ with Stage 1 (33 Raptor, $I_{sp} = 327\text{s}$) and Stage 2 (6 Raptor, $I_{sp} = 380\text{s}$).
   - Liftoff thrust: $74.5\text{ MN}$ ($T/W \approx 1.54$).
   - Max Q: $36.89\text{ kPa}$ at $T+68.5\text{s}$ at $h = 13.8\text{ km}$, $v = 492\text{ m/s}$, Mach $1.64$.
   - Hot-staging separation: $T+162.0\text{s}$ at $h = 67.2\text{ km}$, $v = 1,750\text{ m/s}$.
   - Upper stage SECO: $T+522.35\text{s}$ at $h = 150.2\text{ km}$, $v_{inertial} = 7,698.6\text{ m/s}$.

2. **Orbital Ephemeris (Phase 5 J2 Propagator vs. NASA GMAT Benchmark):**
   - Propagator: Earth gravitational potential with $J_2 = 1.08263 \times 10^{-3}$ oblateness perturbation.
   - Benchmark verification: Validated against NASA General Mission Analysis Tool (GMAT R2026a).
   - Semi-major axis residual: $8.91\text{ m}$ ($0.000136\%$ difference over 1 full orbit).
   - Orbit eccentricity: $e = 0.0012$, inclination $i = 26.3^\circ$.

3. **Hypersonic Entry & Aerothermodynamics (Phase 6 & 9 Solvers):**
   - Stagnation convective heat flux: Sutton-Graves relation $\dot{q}_s = k_{SG} \sqrt{\rho / R_n} v^3$ ($k_{SG} = 1.7415 \times 10^{-4}\text{ kg}^{0.5}/\text{m}$).
   - Peak stagnation heat flux: $0.794\text{ MW/m}^2$ ($794\text{ kW/m}^2$) at $h = 64.2\text{ km}$, $v = 7,240\text{ m/s}$.
   - Radiative equilibrium surface temperature: $T_{surface} = 1,330\text{ K}$ ($1,057^\circ\text{C}$).
   - 1D TDMA transient heat conduction through hexagonal silica tiles with RTV silicone bondline: Peak bondline temperature $377.1\text{ K}$ ($104.0^\circ\text{C}$), providing $+156.0\text{ K}$ margin below the $533.15\text{ K}$ structural adhesive degradation threshold.

4. **Synthetic Terminal Landing Dynamics (Phase 7 Reusable Landing Solver):**
   - Isolated simulation demonstrating belly-flop aerodynamic descent ($AoA = 90^\circ$, $C_D = 1.85$).
   - Dynamic flip maneuver initiated at $h = 500\text{ m}$, $v = 85\text{ m/s}$ with 3-to-1 Raptor throttle modulation down to $v = 0.2\text{ m/s}$ at touchdown.
   - Strictly isolated in dedicated synthetic profile with unmistakable amber warning banner.
