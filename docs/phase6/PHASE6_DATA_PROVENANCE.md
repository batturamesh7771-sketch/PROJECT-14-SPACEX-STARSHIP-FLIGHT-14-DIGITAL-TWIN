## 1. Provenance Classification Taxonomy

Every parameter, initial condition, boundary constraint, aerodynamic coefficient, and physical model incorporated into Phase 6 is tagged with one of six authoritative engineering provenance categories:

1. `PUBLIC_FACT`: Directly verified from published official launch regulatory filings, FAA environmental assessments, official SpaceX public webcasts, and international standard physical constants (CODATA / NOAA / NASA).
2. `DERIVED_PUBLIC`: Analytically derived from public observations or documented geometric dimensions (e.g., vehicle 9.0 m diameter cross-sectional area).
3. `MODEL_ASSUMPTION`: Engineering judgment or literature baseline where proprietary vehicle parameters are unavailable (e.g., high-angle hypersonic drag coefficient $C_d \approx 1.50$, nose effective curvature radius $R_n \approx 4.5\,\text{m}$).
4. `SYNTHETIC_SCENARIO`: Constructed mission scenario for verification, guidance evaluation, and dispersion analysis (e.g., target splashdown coordinates at $26.50^\circ\,\text{N}$, $162.00^\circ\,\text{W}$).
5. `SIMULATION_OUTPUT`: Numerically integrated values generated by the Phase 6 equations of motion (e.g., peak deceleration, peak dynamic pressure, total heat load).
6. `VALIDATION_REFERENCE`: Independent reference models or authoritative standard formulations (e.g., US Standard Atmosphere 1976 NOAA-S/T 76-1562, NASA TR R-376 Sutton-Graves correlation).

## 3. Strict Non-Fabrication and Anti-Hallucination Boundaries

1. **Proprietary Flight Software Exclusion:** No proprietary SpaceX GNC flight algorithms, internal gain schedules, control-allocation mixer matrices, or actuator models are utilized. Guidance is implemented strictly as an open, generic proportional-derivative bank-angle modulation controller.
2. **Proprietary TPS Temperatures:** Radiative equilibrium wall temperatures ($T_{\text{wall}}$) are theoretical surface equilibrium estimates under a non-catalytic gray-body radiation assumption ($\epsilon = 0.85$); they do not represent measured thermocouple or pyrometer data from actual SpaceX flights.
3. **Recovery Certification:** Terminal trajectory impact at sea level ($h = 0\,\text{m}$) represents a physical point of ballistic surface intersection and does not model vehicle structural breakup, terminal aerodynamic flip maneuvers, landing burn relight, or soft ocean touchdown survivability.
