## 1. Executive Summary

This audit establishes the formal technical record of observed anomalies, screenshot symptoms, root cause diagnoses, code locations, and verification criteria for the Starship Flight 14 Cinematic Mission Replay (`replay.html`). Every defect identified by user visual evidence and engineering directive is cataloged with verified evidence.

## 3. NASA-STD-7009B Credibility Assessment

In compliance with **NASA-STD-7009B (Standard for Models and Simulations)**:
1. **Verification of Model Implementation:** Mathematical solvers and 3D visualization transforms must be verified against analytical standards and free from interpolation/falsy evaluation errors.
2. **Data Pedigree & Provenance:** Every displayed telemetry value must identify its physical formula, reference coordinate system, and underlying solver module.
3. **Disclosed Limitations:** Trajectory segments reconstructed from public SpaceX telemetry milestones are transparently flagged as `RECONSTRUCTED — PUBLIC FLIGHT DATA` rather than claimed as proprietary onboard telemetry.
